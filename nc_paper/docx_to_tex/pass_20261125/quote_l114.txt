The Ross sector instead stands out in the last interglacial, where the surface ocean between 60 and 75°S is 0.97~K warmer than in PI and sits 2.72~K above the freezing point, against changes below 0.3~K in the other two sectors. Winter ice concentration there falls by 0.19, an order of magnitude more than the Weddell or Ad\'elie response. In that state the wind can export ice efficiently but the water is too far from freezing for the exported ice to be replaced, so the sector loses ice cover and gains exposure to the atmosphere.
