The winter mixed layer of the pre-industrial run has the same large-scale pattern as the ORAS5 reanalysis but is too deep in the interior of the Weddell gyre. Compared with satellite observations for 1979–2008, the simulated September sea ice extent is about half of the observed value. Most of the deficit is in the Weddell sector, where the simulated ice edge lies about 10° of latitude too far south, and almost all of the ice melts in summer.
