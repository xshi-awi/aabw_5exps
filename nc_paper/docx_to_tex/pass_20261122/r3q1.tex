What differs is where dense water is made. In the model, PI dense water forms by open-ocean convection in the interior of the Weddell gyre near 68°S, not by the sequence of ice shelf melt, dense shelf water formation in coastal polynyas, and downslope overflow that operates in the real ocean (Orsi et al., 1999; Toggweiler and Samuels, 1995). Because open-ocean convection exposes the water column to the atmosphere over a large area, it recruits an excessive thermal contribution and produces water that is not dense enough, which is precisely the offset in density class documented above.
