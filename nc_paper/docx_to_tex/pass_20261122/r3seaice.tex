Our new comparison with satellite sea ice, shown in the reply to Reviewer 1 (Figure~\ref{fR:seaice}), points the same way. In the pre-industrial run the Weddell gyre is nearly free of ice in September, where satellites show close to full ice cover, and the simulated winter ice extent is about half of the observed. This open water is where the model convects. It also lets the atmosphere cool the ocean over a larger area, which is consistent with the strong thermal contribution in our pre-industrial transformation.
