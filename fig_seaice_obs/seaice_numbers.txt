Southern Hemisphere sea ice: AWI-ESM2 vs OSI SAF CDR v3.0
Written by fig_seaice_obs/plot_seaice_obs.py. Extent = area of cells/nodes with
concentration >= 15%; area = sum of concentration x cell area. Units 1e6 km2.
Model: native FESOM2 mesh, nod_area level 0 (m2; mesh_core2 ocean total 364.4 e6 km2).
Obs: Sea Ice Concentration Climate Data Record Version 3 (SMMR, SSM/I, and SSMIS) from the EUMETSAT OSI SAF, osi-450-a v3.0, doi:10.15770/EUM_SAF_OSI_0013.
Obs grid: EASE2 25 km LAEA 432x432, cell area 625.0 km2 (from xc/yc spacing).
Model PI: pi_age model years 2300-2399 (100 yr, pi/100years/a_ice_mergetime.nc).
Monthly values are computed from monthly-mean concentration fields for both model
and obs. "mean" = mean over years of each year's monthly extent; "clim field" =
extent of the multi-year mean concentration field.
Obs days per month: before Aug 1987 every other day; Apr-May 1986 have no data;
Jun 1986 has 4 days, Dec 1987 2 days, Jan 1988 19 days. All available days are used.
